# Reefer Pre-Trip Inspection (PTI) & Compliance Checklist

## 1. Pre-Trip Inspection Requirements
- Every empty reefer container must pass a full Pre-Trip Inspection (PTI) within 7 days prior to gate-out for cold-chain loading.
- Inspect condenser coils, evaporator fan amperage, door gasket seal integrity, and fresh-air vent closure.

## 2. Probe Calibration & Record Retention
- Supply and return air temperature probes must be calibrated every **6 months** using a certified reference thermometer with accuracy within ±0.2°C.
- Signed PTI logs and excursion audit sheets must be retained in the terminal compliance archive for **5 years**.

## 3. External Systems Outside This Helpdesk
- Remote IoT reefer temperature setpoint overrides require direct authorization in the vessel/terminal SCADA telemetry console and cannot be executed from this documentation assistant.
- Electronic customs clearance manifest filings must be submitted directly through the national customs single-window portal.
