HAZARDOUS CARGO EXCEPTIONS & BROWNOUT PRIORITY RULES

1. IMDG Segregation Near Reefer Power Banks:
- Containers carrying IMDG Class 2.1 (Flammable Gases) or Class 3 (Flammable Liquids) must maintain at least 6 meters horizontal segregation from energized 440V reefer power racks.
- Combined Reefer + Class 1 Explosives stacking is strictly prohibited in all coastal cold-chain zones.

2. Shore Power Brownout Priority Tiers:
- Tier 1 (Highest Priority, never shed): Active Pharmaceutical (Class P) reefers.
- Tier 2 (Protected): Frozen Seafood (Class F) units when internal temp exceeds -16°C.
- Tier 3 (Cycle-eligible up to 60 minutes): Chilled Produce (Class C) containers during peak grid load shedding.

3. Emergency Backup Generator Cutover:
- Diesel backup generators must automatically pick up Tier 1 and Tier 2 reefer loads within 45 seconds of shore-grid voltage drop below 380V.
- Fuel reserves must support a minimum of 72 continuous hours of Tier 1 and Tier 2 operation.
