IT SUPPORT SLA AND LOANER GUIDELINES

Response Time SLAs:
- Severity P1 (Critical outage affecting multiple users or servers): 30 minutes initial response, 4 hours target resolution.
- Severity P2 (Single user blocked from primary workstation): 4 hours initial response, 1 business day target resolution.
- Severity P3 (Accessory request or non-blocking issue): 1 business day initial response, 3 business days target resolution.

Loaner Equipment:
- Employees with a confirmed P1 or P2 hardware failure may borrow a standard loaner laptop for up to 14 calendar days while awaiting repair or replacement.
- Loaners must be returned within 2 business days of receiving replacement hardware.
