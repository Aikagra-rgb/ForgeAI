# Corporate IT Asset & Replacement Policy

## 1. Warranty & Replacement Eligibility
- Hardware assets whose `warranty_expiry` date is earlier than today's date are classified as **Out of Warranty** and eligible for immediate standard replacement.
- Assets still within warranty require a P1 or P2 hardware diagnostic ticket before replacement is authorized.

## 2. Bulk Order Discounts
- Orders of **1 to 4 units** pay standard catalogue `unit_price_usd`.
- Orders of **5 to 9 units** receive a **10% bulk discount** on the subtotal.
- Orders of **10 or more units** receive a **15% bulk discount** on the subtotal.
- A flat USD 75 staging and imaging fee applies per order regardless of quantity.

## 3. Purchase Approvals
- Orders exceeding USD 5,000 require VP Finance sign-off.
- Automated Jira ticket submission is not integrated into documentation tools; coordinators must submit Jira purchase approval tickets manually in the corporate Jira portal.
